Attachment tier shifts
The addition of Blue/Violet lasers or Light barrels attachments will each decrease your tier by -1 (improves accuracy) Having "ADS Accuracy While Moving" penalty attachments increases your tier by +1 (decreases accuracy). By default, only the +0/base moving case is shown. Burst/tap-fire mode also resets this to +0 only.

Burst compare only affects the spread compare plot. In burst mode, the main shots slider becomes the number of bursts, and the graph still shows pre-shot spread only. The burst wait slider adds extra delay beyond normal shot cadence, so 0.00 s matches the fastest possible re-start.
Standing is plotted once per weapon as a solid line. Moving cases use dashed lines. Weapon A and Weapon B use different marker symbols and colors. The effective spread increase overlay shows the shot-to-shot net increase in pre-shot spread, which helps reveal how much a weapon is actually blooming after decay between rounds.

Weapon data from Sym.gg weapon comparison tool. Refreshed 2 Sep 2026 for the current BF6 1.4.2.0 / Sym.gg data pass.

Burst Delay:
M16A4 = 33 ms
BROD 3 = 33 ms
SL9 = 25 ms
GRT-BC = 35 ms

Other burst-mode guns can reach their full-auto fire rate with a burst delay of 0 ms.
